Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what personal data Rilly (“we”, “us”) handles, why, and the choices you have. It covers two groups of people:

  • Our customers: the business owners, teammates and agency clients who use Rilly. For their data, Rilly is the controller.
  • Our customers’ contacts: the people who visit their pages, sign up, book, buy, or receive their emails and texts. For this data, the business using Rilly is the controller and Rilly processes it on their behalf. If you’re one of these people, please contact that business first; we’ll help them respond.

1. What we collect

From our customers

  • Account details: name, email, password (stored only as a secure hash), business name and settings.
  • Billing details: your plan and payment status. Card details are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • What you create in Rilly: pages, emails, products, files, follow-ups and the contacts you add or import.
  • Usage and device information: pages of the app you use, sign-in times, IP address and browser type, used to keep accounts secure and the Service working.

For our customers, about their contacts

  • What contacts give on forms, bookings and checkouts: name, email, phone, address, messages, birthdays and consent to texts (with the wording they agreed to).
  • Activity: page visits, sign-ups, purchases, bookings, email opens and clicks, downloads, replies, and where they came from (for example a link or an ad).
  • Messages: emails, texts, website chats and missed calls in the business’s inbox.

2. How we use it

  • To run the Service: host pages, send the emails and texts our customers set up, take bookings and payments, and show results.
  • To keep it safe: prevent abuse and spam, protect accounts and meet carrier and email-provider rules.
  • To support and improve Rilly, and to send our customers service emails (for example receipts, security notices and important changes). You can opt out of product tips at any time.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax and accounting records.

We don’t sell personal data, and we don’t use our customers’ contacts for our own marketing. We don’t sell, rent or share mobile numbers or text-message consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

3. AI drafting

When a customer asks Rilly to draft copy or suggest page titles, the relevant text is sent to our AI provider to produce the draft. It is used only to create the response, under terms that don’t allow the provider to train its models on it.

4. Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run Rilly, under contracts that protect the data:

  • Hosting and database (Netlify, Supabase)
  • Payments (Stripe)
  • Email delivery (Resend) and text messaging and calls (Twilio)
  • AI drafting (Anthropic)

We may also share data if the law requires it, to protect people’s safety or our rights, or as part of a merger or sale of the business (with this policy still applying).

5. Cookies

The Rilly app uses a cookie to keep you signed in. Pages our customers publish use a first-party cookie to remember where a visitor came from (so the business can see which links bring sign-ups and sales) and, for members and partners, to keep them signed in. Businesses can also add their own analytics or ad pixels to their pages; those are under the business’s control and policies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep data while an account is active. After an account is closed we delete its data within 90 days, except billing and legal records we must keep longer. Backups roll off on a regular schedule. Contacts who unsubscribe are kept on a suppression list so they aren’t emailed again.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, passwords are hashed, access is limited to people who need it, and links in emails (like download links) are signed so they can’t be guessed. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we’ll tell you without undue delay.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to how it’s used. Our customers can do most of this themselves in Settings and People. To make a request, email hello@getrilly.com. If you’re a contact of a business that uses Rilly, ask that business first; we’ll support them.

9. International transfers

Rilly and its providers are based in the United States. Where data moves from the EU, UK or elsewhere, we rely on safeguards such as the standard contractual clauses.

10. Children

Rilly is for businesses and isn’t meant for children under 16. We don’t knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

We’ll post changes here and update the date above. For significant changes we’ll email account owners before they apply.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: hello@getrilly.com.
