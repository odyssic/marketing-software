Questions

Do I need a card to start?

No. You get the full product free for 14 days. Add a card only if you want to keep going.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Nothing is deleted and your published pages keep working. Editing and sending pause until you choose a plan.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. There’s no contract. Cancel in Settings in a couple of clicks, and you can export your people as a spreadsheet whenever you like.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, for your website and pages, and for sending email (like hello@yourbusiness.com). Rilly walks you through it.

How do I take payments?

Connect your Stripe account and money goes straight to you. Until you do, checkouts run in test mode so you can try everything.

Can I text my customers?

Yes, in the US. Get a local number in a minute; texts and calls land in your inbox. Carriers ask for a few business details before marketing texts go out.

Can I bring my existing contacts?

Yes. Upload a spreadsheet, paste a list, or even take a photo of a paper sign-up sheet.

I run an agency. How does pricing work?

$29/month covers your first business, and each client business is $19/month more. Clients you invite never pay Rilly themselves.
